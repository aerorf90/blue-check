Player consent comes first

A PC check should be transparent. The player must understand the process and agree before it begins.

  • The player sees what is checked and consents before scanning.
  • Do not collect documents, passwords, browser history, or screenshots.
  • Only the staff account authorized for the check should access its report.
  • Reports are configured for automatic deletion after 30 days.

FAQ fréquentes

Can the player see the report?

Non. Il voit seulement que la vérification est terminée. Reports n'apparaît que dans votre dashboard.

What if the player declines?

The scan should not start without consent. Follow your community rules for next steps.

Does the score prove cheating?

No. It is a triage indicator. Review the underlying evidence before taking action.

Can any tool detect every cheat?

No tool can guarantee detection of every cheat, especially renamed or memory-only tools. Coverage depends on signatures and testing.

How long does a key last?

For the purchased duration, starting when the key is activated.

What data is retained?

Reports, supprimé automatiquement après 30 jours. Le détail est dans la politique de confidentialité.
